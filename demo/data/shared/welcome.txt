Welcome to the Northwind demo data.

Everything in this bucket is invented for this demo. The company, the people and the numbers do not
exist.

Three folders:
  shared/       everyone with a demo group may read it
  finance/      only the group demo-finance
  engineering/  only the group demo-engineering

Who may read what is decided by RustFS, not by the gateway in front of it. Open a link to a folder you
have no right to: the refusal comes from the object store itself.
